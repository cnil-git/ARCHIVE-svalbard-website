“What is Project Svalbard?” Section

Industrial and Bold, Project Svalbard is a unique virtual music venue in VRChat that is set inside an alternate, virtual version of the real-life Svalbard Global Seed Vault. Set 400 feet deep inside the Svalbardian mountainscape, lies “The Vault,” Project Svalbard’s prime venue area. The Vault is an open venue ripe with industrial design aspects and a unique layout. The layout of Project Svalbard is one of uniqueness, as on both the left and right sides of the venue area is a catwalk complete with a suspended bridge connecting the two. This catwalk area, suspended above the main floor, isn't just for show, as it allows access to the two VIP accessible areas in Project Svalbard’s main venue. These VIP areas, sunken into the left and right side walls of the venue, consist of many things, such as a bar, lounge, and more. Project Svalbard is also a venue that is open to small creators, big creators, and everyone in between. Whether you are big or small, you will be welcomed to play at Project Svalbard as we love giving an avenue for the new, old, and up and coming DJs  and artist’s to play and show their hard work off.
